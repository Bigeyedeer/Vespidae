Most wasps are solitary. Of the tens of thousands of described species, only a small fraction live in colonies at all.
Only female wasps can sting. The sting is a modified egg-laying tube, so males never had one to begin with.
Paper wasps really do make paper: they scrape plant fibre, chew it with saliva, and spread it into a nest by hand.
A paper wasp colony starts with a single overwintered queen. Everything you command grew out of one insect.
Adult wasps live on sugar, but their larvae need meat. The colony hunts caterpillars to feed the brood.
Wasp larvae pay their carers back: they release a sweet droplet the adults drink in exchange for food.
A single social wasp colony can take thousands of caterpillars and aphids off surrounding plants in one season.
Wasps pollinate too. They visit flowers for nectar and carry pollen from bloom to bloom while they do it.
Figs and fig wasps cannot live without each other. The wasp pollinates the fig; the fig raises the wasp's young.
Paper wasps build open combs with no outer envelope. You can see every cell of the nest from below.
German wasps build enclosed nests, often underground, and can keep a colony going far longer than a native paper wasp.
The European paper wasp reached South Africa's Western Cape in the late 2000s and has been spreading ever since.
The German wasp has been established in the Western Cape since the 1970s and raids honeybee hives for protein.
Invasive wasps do not just compete with native insects, they eat them. Predation is how they clear ground.
Species invasion is one of the largest drivers of biodiversity loss worldwide, alongside habitat destruction.
The Cape Floral Region holds around nine thousand plant species, and roughly seven in ten grow nowhere else on Earth.
Fynbos is built to burn. Many of its plants need fire to release or germinate their seeds.
Wasps recognise their nestmates by smell. A colony carries a shared chemical signature.
Some paper wasps recognise each other by face, telling individual nestmates apart by their markings.
Wasps cool their nests by carrying in water and fanning it with their wings, like a living air conditioner.
An alarmed wasp releases a pheromone that recruits the rest of the nest. Swatting one near a nest is a bad plan.
Unfertilised wasp eggs hatch into males. A queen chooses her sons and daughters as she lays.
Spider wasps hunt spiders many times their own size, paralyse them, and seal them in a burrow as living larval food.
Killing a native wasp nest removes free pest control. Identify before you intervene.
Not every wasp at your picnic is a pest. Most of the ones you meet were never a threat to anything but a caterpillar.
Native and invasive paper wasps can look almost identical in flight. The markings only separate them up close.
